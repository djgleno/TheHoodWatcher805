SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 22, 2026 • CENTRAL COAST EDITION • PACIFIC TIME

Storm recovery, Paso Robles land decisions and a dangerous surf warning

Today's local briefing covers developments in Paso Robles, Templeton, San Luis Obispo County and the coast. Stories below summarize verified published reports and official releases from recent days; the date of each underlying event is stated separately. This is curated reporting, not original interviews.

Lead story • San Luis Obispo • Emergency response

San Luis Obispo confirms local emergency after Nov. 18–19 storms

The City Council confirmed an emergency proclamation on Friday, Nov. 20, after intense rain, flooding, wind and damage to public infrastructure. The designation supports ongoing recovery efforts and may help the city access disaster resources. It is a city emergency declaration; it does not mean every neighborhood or every county community is under an evacuation order.

Reporting: KSBY · Nov. 21 • City news release republished by Edhat · Nov. 20

Active coastal warning — Nov. 22: The National Weather Service has a high surf advisory for San Luis Obispo County beaches from Sunday at 6 a.m. until Thursday, Nov. 26, at 3 p.m. Dangerous breaking waves and strong rip currents can make beaches, jetties and harbor entrances hazardous. See the National Weather Service advisory for updates before visiting the coast.

Development · Paso Robles

City Council approves The Landing at former Boys School

On Nov. 17, council members voted 5-0 to approve major project entitlements and environmental review for the roughly 140-acre site at Airport and Dry Creek roads. Plans envision approximately 1.6 million square feet of industrial and commercial development. Zoning and development-agreement ordinances are due back for a second reading Dec. 1.

Source: Paso Robles Daily News · Nov. 20

Public land · Paso Robles

Pioneer Park skate park remains central to property debate

Speakers urged the city to retain the skate park while officials consider proposals for approximately 1.65 acres of Pioneer Park. City Manager Chris Huot said proposals will be assessed for whether they preserve the skate park or fund its relocation. The reported proposal deadline is Jan. 19, 2027, at 5 p.m. No sale was announced in the Nov. 17 meeting report.

Source: Council reporting · Nov. 20 • City Pioneer Park information

Elections · North County

Paso Robles results remain unofficial as ballot canvass continues

The county posted updated unofficial Nov. 3 election results on Nov. 20. Recent returns show Faith Frankel ahead in the Paso Robles mayoral race and Measure G-26 below the 55% approval threshold. These are not certified outcomes; readers should consult the county's newest results before drawing final conclusions.

Source: SLO County Clerk-Recorder · updated Nov. 20

Earthquake · North County

Magnitude 5.0 quake near Pinnacles felt around Paso Robles

A magnitude 5.0 earthquake struck near Pinnacles in San Benito County at 10:49 p.m. on Nov. 19. Residents reported weak shaking in Paso Robles, Atascadero, Templeton and San Miguel. As of the Nov. 20 report, no damage had been reported in San Luis Obispo County.

Source: Paso Robles Daily News / USGS · Nov. 20

Jobs · Countywide

County unemployment rate falls to 4.3% for October

State employment figures reported Nov. 21 show San Luis Obispo County's unemployment rate declining from 4.6% in September to 4.3% in October, a third consecutive monthly decrease. The rate is a monthly labor-market measure, not a count of newly created permanent jobs.

Source: CalCoastNews / California EDD · Nov. 21

Community safety · Countywide

$495,000 in grants for car-seat, bike and pedestrian safety

The California Office of Traffic Safety awarded San Luis Obispo County Public Health $275,000 for child passenger safety and $220,000 for bicycle and pedestrian safety. The funding covers car-seat checks, helmet distribution and e-bike education through September 2027.

Source: Paso Robles Daily News / County Public Health · Nov. 20

Courts · Pismo Beach

Jury returns verdict in 2023 Pismo Beach Pier shooting case

A county jury convicted Daniel Jacob Santillana of assault with a semiautomatic firearm in connection with the 2023 incident in which Gustavo Aquino died. Prosecutors described Santillana as an aider and abettor; the juvenile shooter was handled in separate proceedings. Sentencing is scheduled for Dec. 15, according to KSBY.

Source: KSBY / District Attorney · Nov. 20

Civic participation · Templeton

Templeton seeks residents to serve on district committees

Templeton Community Services District is recruiting residents for standing committees overseeing administration and finance, utilities, and other district functions. Applicants must live within district boundaries and be at least 16; the reported application deadline is Jan. 8, 2027.

Source: Paso Robles Daily News · Nov. 21

Arts & community · Paso Robles

Three permanent light sculptures brighten Downtown City Park

Sensorio and the city unveiled 'Gift of Light' on Nov. 17. Three illuminated geometric works by HYBYCOZO stand near the Carnegie Library and are intended to glow year-round, under a maintenance arrangement presently running through 2029.

Source: Paso Robles Daily News · Nov. 19

Upcoming · Paso Robles

Downtown lighting ceremony set for Friday, Nov. 27

The Downtown Paso Robles Main Street Association plans a free holiday lighting celebration from 5 to 6:30 p.m. in Downtown City Park, with caroling, cocoa and seasonal activities. The event is upcoming, not an event that happened today.

Source: Paso Robles Daily News · Nov. 21
