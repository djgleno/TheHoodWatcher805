Thanksgiving on the Central Coast

King tides and high surf brought holiday closures along the coast, San Luis Obispo lifted a drinking-water notice, and Paso Robles gets ready to light up City Park on Friday.

Lead story • Coast

King tides and high surf brought Thanksgiving pier closures

High surf and coastal flooding warnings covered Thanksgiving morning, when breaking waves of 15 to 22 feet were forecast. Daily San Luis Obispo reported that county piers closed during the warnings. Check current advisories and local reopening notices before heading to the waterfront.

Daily San Luis Obispo · Nov. 26 • National Weather Service Los Angeles/Oxnard

Latest local news

Public utilities · San Luis Obispo

City lifts drinking-water notice for Serrano and Patricia areas

A Nov. 25 county alert says the city and State Water Board confirmed tap water was safe for normal use after flushing and testing. The notice applied to customers served by the Serrano storage tank. Edhat reported that it covered 516 addresses.

ReadySLO · Nov. 25 • Edhat · Nov. 26

Community · Paso Robles

Paso Robles planned its 42nd annual Thanksgiving meal at Centennial Park

Organizers scheduled the free sit-down meal at Centennial Park for Thanksgiving afternoon. Volunteers also arranged deliveries for homebound residents who requested them in advance.

Paso Robles Daily News · Nov. 23

Community · Santa Maria

Salvation Army canceled its annual Thanksgiving dinner

KSBY reported that the Santa Maria Salvation Army called off the meal after a refrigerator broke and donations fell short. Organizers told KSBY they would use the turkeys they did receive for Meals on Wheels and to help people experiencing homelessness.

KSBY · Nov. 24

Friday · Paso Robles

Downtown City Park lights up Friday at 5 p.m.

Paso Robles’ Main Street Association plans a free lighting ceremony from 5 to 6:30 p.m. on Nov. 27. The program includes candlelight caroling, coffee and cocoa.

Downtown Paso Robles Main Street Association

Friday · San Luis Obispo

Mission Plaza holiday lights open Friday

Downtown SLO’s Light Up the Plaza kicks off on Nov. 27. Its Shop Local Weekend runs Friday and Saturday, and the downtown holiday parade is scheduled for Dec. 4 at 7 p.m.
